✦ Our Story

Born from a love of beautiful things

Lavender Gleam started in a small apartment studio in Bautista, Pangasinan. What began as a hobby — bending wire, setting stones, gifting pieces to friends — quickly became something more. Word spread. Orders came in. And a brand was born.

We believe jewelry should be personal. Not mass-produced or trend-chasing, but quiet and considered — made to last decades and be passed down. Every Lavender Gleam piece is designed to tell a story that's uniquely yours.
